Workbench (build it with the hammer) — craft guns, ammo, a Tank and an F-4 Phantom
Hit trees for wood, rocks for stone and wrecked cars for scrap. Every night a horde attacks your base and it gets bigger each day. Place a Bed to respawn at your base. Level up to unlock Spikes (Lv 2), Stone Walls (Lv 3) and Turrets (Lv 5). Drive cars across the huge map (refuel at gas stations or with fuel cans). Grab the daily airdrop for the best loot. Hunt deer and cook the meat — don't starve. Watch out for zombie dogs, acid spitters, exploding bloaters and the giant Abomination that comes every 3rd night. Guns come in Rare, Epic and Legendary. Watch for crashed helicopters, the wandering trader and the Blood Moon.
